# IDE Guide Money Engine

## Pipeline

1. Discover developer tools and trends.
2. Score topics by demand, buyer intent, competition, and verified affiliate availability.
3. Select three high-opportunity topics.
4. Research and verify product facts and official monetization terms.
5. Generate an AMP Blogger article, comparison/decision guide, YouTube Short plan, long-form video plan, social hook, and thumbnail brief.
6. Add affiliate CTAs only for entries whose status is `verified` in `content/affiliate_programs.json`.
7. Keep `discovery_only` programs out of monetized copy.
8. Publish through connected accounts when credentials and platform access are configured.
9. Track views, clicks, conversions, commissions, and payouts in the revenue wallet.
10. Reinvest attention into topics with the strongest verified conversion results.

## Maestro-inspired layer

Use the public repository's ideas as architectural inspiration, not copied code: scene planning, reusable characters/presenters, multi-scene video specs, local-first generation, and workflow automation.

## Revenue channels

- Verified software/SaaS affiliate programs
- YouTube monetization
- Blogger/AdSense where eligible
- Sponsored placements with clear disclosure
- Premium developer guides, templates, and setup packs
- Direct B2B leads for software and automation services

## Payout wallet

`content/revenue_wallet.json` is an internal accounting ledger only. It is not a bank account, payment wallet, or custody service. Real payout accounts must be opened by the owner with the relevant provider and credentials must never be committed to Git.

## Safety and trust rules

Never invent affiliate URLs, commissions, cookie periods, program availability, earnings, or payment status. Never store passwords, API secrets, or payment tokens in the repository.

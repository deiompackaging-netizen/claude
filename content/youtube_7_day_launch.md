# DEIOM Official: 7-Day Revenue Launch

## Objective
Use YouTube as the audience engine and convert qualified viewers into legitimate digital-product sales and business leads while the channel works toward YPP eligibility.

## Publishing cadence
- 1 useful long-form video per day for 7 days.
- 2 Shorts cut from each long-form video when practical.
- Every video has one primary CTA, not a pile of unrelated links.

## Description structure
1. One-sentence value proposition.
2. Primary product link.
3. Free useful resource when available.
4. Business/contact link for serious B2B enquiries.
5. Affiliate disclosure when an affiliate link is present.
6. Short channel description.

## Important
Do not buy views/subscribers, use engagement bots, scrape private data, spam comments, or claim guaranteed earnings. Those shortcuts can damage monetization eligibility and the business itself.

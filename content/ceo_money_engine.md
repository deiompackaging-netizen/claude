# DEIOM CEO Money Engine

## Objective
Build a legitimate, measurable online-revenue system around DEIOM's existing assets. The system prioritizes revenue paths that can produce a sale or qualified business lead before relying on platform ad revenue.

## Priority order
1. Digital products with immediate checkout.
2. B2B solar and automation lead generation.
3. Verified software affiliate content.
4. YouTube + Instagram audience growth.
5. YouTube ad revenue as a secondary layer.

## Why this order
Ad revenue requires audience scale. A product or B2B service can produce revenue with a much smaller audience. Therefore every content asset should have a commercial purpose, not merely views.

## Product ladder
- $7: AI IDE Starter Guide
- $12: Developer Productivity Template Pack
- $19: Solar Quotation Automation Starter Pack
- $49-$199: implementation/setup service
- Custom: solar quotation system, printing ERP/prepress software, automation and integration work

## Daily operating loop
1. Select three high-buyer-intent topics.
2. Produce one useful article, one long-form video plan and three Shorts/Reels angles.
3. Attach one relevant product CTA and one B2B CTA.
4. Publish only original/helpful content and disclose affiliate relationships.
5. Track clicks, leads, sales, revenue, refund rate and conversion rate.
6. Kill weak topics and double down on topics producing qualified leads or sales.

## 30-day targets
These are operating targets, not income guarantees:
- 90 short-form content concepts
- 30 commercial articles
- 12 long-form YouTube videos
- 3 paid digital products listed
- 100 qualified B2B prospects researched
- 20 personalized outreach messages sent per working day, only to relevant prospects and with opt-out handling
- 1 weekly revenue review

## Hard rules
- No fake views, clicks, subscribers, reviews, leads or sales.
- No spam blasting.
- No invented affiliate commissions or payout terms.
- No copied/stolen content.
- No credentials, OTPs, PINs, recovery codes or bank details in GitHub.
- Do not promise a specific income amount.

## External actions still requiring the owner's account
- Create/verify Gumroad products and payout bank account.
- Authorize YouTube/Google OAuth for publishing and analytics if automation is later enabled.
- Authorize Instagram/Meta if publishing or advertising automation is enabled.
- Approve and send business outreach from the owner's email account.

## Success metric
The engine is successful only when traffic turns into measurable qualified leads, product purchases, or service contracts. Views alone are not revenue.

## YouTube
Use DEIOM Official for original tutorials, reviews, comparisons, and demonstrations. Current YouTube ad-revenue eligibility is 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 qualified public watch hours in 12 months or 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days. YouTube also has earlier-access features in eligible regions at lower thresholds.
